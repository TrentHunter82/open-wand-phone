CALIBRATION
Choose your projected display, mark your comfortable left and right aim twice, then test the proposed reach with your normal clutch. The phone reports when the cursor reaches each edge. Nothing saves until you tap Apply. Your steadiness, touch guard, and vertical direction settings stay as they are. Undo calibration restores your previous reach.
FEEL
Hz added to the cutoff for every 100 deg/s of hand speed. This was broken until now: the old value was 57 times too small to do anything, so the deadzone was carrying all the smoothing. Your value converted to the same behaviour, which is near zero. Try 3 to 6 to get what the setting was always meant to do.
Dim = raw rate. Bright = filtered. Grey band = deadzone. Hold the clutch and keep the hand still: the bright line must sit inside the band.
TOUCH
Tap-hold to drag: tap once, then touch again and keep the thumb down. The left button latches, so you drag with the wand. Lift to drop.
HAPTICS
Toggle the switch above and you feel the tick iOS gives a page. That is the only way to get one: a real touch on a real switch. Code cannot fire it, and nothing can fire it at press. So every control holds a hidden switch, your own touch plays the tick, and the tick lands when you lift. Press is fed by the top-edge flash and, if you want it, the click below.
iOS gives a page no way to play a haptic at press. The tick needs the switch to travel, and a resting thumb never moves far enough. So press gets a light flash at the top edge and, if you want it, a short click through the speaker. The Taptic tick still lands on release. The ringer switch mutes the click, and Bluetooth audio delays it.
SCROLL
Two-finger scroll is off by default now. The strip does the same job with one finger, and a second finger on the clutch zone is how Safari decides you asked it to zoom the page.
The strip is the tall bar beside the clutch. Drag it with one finger to scroll. It moves to the left side with the left-hand layout. Two fingers in the clutch zone still scroll as well.
LAYOUT
Lower this to keep the pads clear of Safari's toolbar. At 100 the clutch zone takes the whole middle, as it did before.
Use these if the page changes size. Reset page size puts the scale back. If the amber bar reports a page zoom percentage, only Safari can clear that one: tap aA at the address bar and set 100%.

HOTKEY PAD
Three pads, side by side above the main row. Leave a field empty to drop that pad. Each holds up to four keys joined by +. Names: ctrl, alt, shift, win, f1–f24, a–z, 0–9, esc, tab, space, enter, backspace, delete, insert, home, end, pageup, pagedown, up, down, left, right, and the punctuation names. A red field is not saved.
MORE ACTIONS
Up to three named macros. Separate shortcuts with commas: ctrl+a, ctrl+c. Each shortcut is pressed and released before the next. Maximum 12 steps, with a short pause between steps. These run in the focused PC app; they do not wait for windows to load. Blank steps hide a macro. Changes save when you leave a field. Red fields are not saved.
